# NuPhy Lab 1.2.53

## 中文

- 在功能中心添加「刚刚怎么了」，开启全屏回放后，可用按钮或自定义快捷键保存刚过去的 15 秒。画面在本机缓存，MP4 保存到「影片 / NuPhy Lab / Replays」。关闭回放或锁屏会清空未保存的画面。

在 **功能中心** 添加 **刚刚怎么了**，打开 **全屏回放**。首次使用时，按 macOS 提示授予屏幕录制权限。连接多台显示器时，可在回放关闭时选择要回放的屏幕。

点 **保存最近 15 秒** 可将刚过去的画面保存为无声 MP4。刚开启时，片段长度取决于已有缓存。点 **设置快捷键** 可录制单键、组合键或鼠标侧键。使用全局快捷键需要辅助功能权限。

在 **最近保存** 可播放片段或在访达中显示文件。全屏回放需要手动开启。关闭回放、锁屏或退出应用会清空尚未保存的画面。

## English

- Add Instant Replay from Feature Center. Turn it on to save the last 15 seconds of your display with a button or a custom shortcut. Silent clips are buffered locally and saved as MP4 files in Movies / NuPhy Lab / Replays. Turning replay off or locking your Mac clears the unsaved buffer.

Add **Instant Replay** in **Feature Center** and turn on **Full-screen replay**. Allow screen recording when macOS prompts you. With multiple displays connected, choose a display while replay is off.

Click **Save last 15 seconds** to save the preceding screen activity as a silent MP4. If you just turned replay on, the clip contains the time available in memory. Click **Set shortcut** to record a key, key combination or mouse side button. The global shortcut requires Accessibility access.

Use **Last saved replay** to play the clip or show it in Finder. Turn replay on when you want to start buffering. Turning it off, locking the Mac or quitting clears unsaved footage.
