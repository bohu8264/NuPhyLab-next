# Feature Center and Library / 功能中心与作品库

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#中文)

## English

### Customize the sidebar

The sidebar starts with Lighting Studio, Key Buddy, AI Assistant and Library. To add another tool, open **Feature Center** at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Add**.

You can remove any of the four defaults. Feature Center, Device and Settings stay available even when you remove every tool.

Upgrading preserves your saved sidebar and its order. **Remove turns the tool and its shortcuts off** and stops its current activity. Works, Pocket contents, shortcut bindings and other preferences remain intact. Re-adding keeps it off until you enable it on its page.

### Dock Scenes

Add **Dock Scenes**, open it from the sidebar, select a scene to preview its pinned apps, then choose **Apply to Dock**. **All** restores the original list saved on first opening. The **Scene shortcuts** switch starts off. Set a combination for each scene, then enable shortcuts. Removing the feature turns shortcuts off; re-add it and enable them when needed. [User guide](DOCK_SCENES.md)

### Browse and continue creating

Library brings lighting and music together. Filter by **All / Lighting / Music**, switch between **My works / Presets**, and search by name.

- Lighting cards show the keyboard pattern. View or continue editing a work, or play it on a connected compatible keyboard. Playing adds Lighting Studio to the sidebar.
- Music cards show a note or drum preview. Preview, edit, duplicate, rename or export a project.
- Choose lighting or music under **New work**, or use a preset to start your own creation.
- If an editor is not in the sidebar, choose **Add Lighting Studio & edit** or **Add Music Mode & edit**. The selected work opens after the editor is added; saving updates that original work.
- The Library button in Music Mode opens this same library with the Music filter selected.

### Save and export

Music recordings and edits autosave on your Mac. Exported `.nuphy-music` projects preserve notes, instruments, drum patterns and audio clips; import them from the Music category. Export WAV audio from the music editor. Deleted music works move to **Recently deleted**, where they can be restored.

Lighting keeps its existing save, import and export controls. Upgrading retains your works and drafts. Organizing the sidebar does not delete personal content.

## 中文

### 整理侧栏

侧栏默认依次显示灯光创作、键盘搭子、AI 助手和作品库。打开侧栏底部的 **功能中心**，找到其他需要的工具并点击 **添加**。

四个默认入口都可以移除。即使移除所有工具，也可以从侧栏进入功能中心、设备和设置。

升级会保留你已保存的侧栏及其顺序。**移除会关闭功能及其快捷键**，同时停止当前功能的运行。作品、口袋内容、快捷键配置和其他偏好仍会保留。重新添加后保持关闭，请在页面按需开启。

### Dock 场景

添加 **Dock 场景** 后，从侧栏打开，选择场景查看固定应用预览，再点击 **应用到 Dock** 切换。首次打开保存的 **全部** 可恢复原固定应用布局。**场景快捷键** 初次使用时关闭；设置各场景的组合键后再开启。移回功能中心会关闭快捷键，重新添加后请按需开启。[使用说明](DOCK_SCENES.md#中文)

### 统一查看与继续创作

作品库汇集灯光和演奏作品。使用 **全部 / 灯光 / 演奏** 筛选类型，在 **我的作品 / 默认预设** 间切换，并按名称搜索。

- 灯光作品显示键盘图案，可查看、继续编辑，或在连接兼容键盘后播放；播放时会添加灯光创作入口。
- 演奏作品显示音符或鼓点预览，可试听、继续编辑、复制、改名和导出工程。
- 从 **新建作品** 选择灯光或演奏，也可以选择预设后以此创作。
- 编辑器尚未出现在侧栏时，选择 **添加灯光创作并编辑** 或 **添加演奏模式并编辑**。添加后会打开所选作品；保存更新原作品。
- 演奏模式中的作品库按钮进入同一个作品库，并自动显示演奏分类。

### 保存与导出

演奏录制和修改自动保存在本机。导出的 `.nuphy-music` 工程保留音符、音色、鼓机图形和声音片段，可从演奏分类导入；WAV 音频在演奏编辑页导出。演奏作品删除后进入 **最近删除**，可以恢复。

灯光作品沿用原有保存、导入与导出功能。升级保留已有作品和草稿；个人内容不会因为整理侧栏而被删除。
