# 功能中心与作品库 / Feature Center and Library

## 中文

### 整理侧栏

新用户默认看到灯光创作、作品库、AI 助手。打开侧栏底部的 **功能中心**，找到需要的功能并点击 **添加**，入口就会出现在侧栏。

灯光游戏、模拟声浪、演奏模式、智能框选、拖拽转换和键盘口袋均可按需添加。默认的三个入口也可以移除。功能中心、设备和设置始终保留，移除所有功能后也能重新添加。

升级会保留已使用或已启用的功能入口。**移除只隐藏入口**，不会删除作品、口袋内容或设置，也不会关闭正在运行的功能。需要停用时，请先进入对应页面关闭功能开关。

### 统一查看与继续创作

作品库汇集灯光和演奏作品。使用 **全部 / 灯光 / 演奏** 筛选类型，在 **我的作品 / 默认预设** 间切换，并按名称搜索。

- 灯光作品显示键盘图案，可查看、继续编辑，或在连接兼容键盘后播放。
- 演奏作品显示音符或鼓点预览，可试听、继续编辑、复制、改名和导出工程。
- 从 **新建作品** 选择灯光或演奏，也可以选择预设后以此创作。
- 编辑器尚未出现在侧栏时，选择 **添加灯光创作并编辑** 或 **添加演奏模式并编辑**。添加后会打开所选作品；保存更新原作品。
- 演奏模式中的作品库按钮进入同一个作品库，并自动显示演奏分类。

### 保存与导出

演奏录制和修改自动保存在本机。导出的 `.nuphy-music` 工程保留音符、音色、鼓机图形和声音片段，可从演奏分类导入；WAV 音频在演奏编辑页导出。演奏作品删除后进入 **最近删除**，可以恢复。

灯光作品沿用原有保存、导入与导出功能。升级保留已有作品和草稿；个人内容不会因为整理侧栏而被删除。

## English

### Customize the sidebar

New users start with Lighting Studio, Library and AI Assistant. Open **Feature Center** at the bottom of the sidebar, find a tool and choose **Add** to show it in the sidebar.

Add Lighting Games, Typing Sounds, Music Mode, Smart Selection, Drag Convert and Keyboard Pocket as needed. You can also remove the three default entries. Feature Center, Device and Settings always stay available, even when all optional entries have been removed.

Upgrading keeps entries for tools you already use or have enabled. **Remove only hides the entry.** It preserves works, Pocket contents and settings, and does not turn off a running feature. To stop a feature, open its page and turn its switch off first.

### Browse and continue creating

Library brings lighting and music together. Filter by **All / Lighting / Music**, switch between **My works / Presets**, and search by name.

- Lighting cards show the keyboard pattern. View or continue editing a work, or play it on a connected compatible keyboard.
- Music cards show a note or drum preview. Preview, edit, duplicate, rename or export a project.
- Choose lighting or music under **New work**, or use a preset to start your own creation.
- If an editor is not in the sidebar, choose **Add Lighting Studio & edit** or **Add Music Mode & edit**. The selected work opens after the editor is added; saving updates that original work.
- The Library button in Music Mode opens this same library with the Music filter selected.

### Save and export

Music recordings and edits autosave on your Mac. Exported `.nuphy-music` projects preserve notes, instruments, drum patterns and audio clips; import them from the Music category. Export WAV audio from the music editor. Deleted music works move to **Recently deleted**, where they can be restored.

Lighting keeps its existing save, import and export controls. Upgrading retains your works and drafts. Organizing the sidebar does not delete personal content.
