# NuPhy Lab 1.2.55

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

- **My Commands.** Save your own instructions in Smart Selection and run them on selected text with a shortcut. Review the original text and instruction, ask a follow-up, and copy the answer when it is ready.
- **Screen Awareness.** Ask a question by typing or speaking. Start with the visible windows on the display under your pointer, or choose a single window or region. Preview the capture before asking, follow up, and copy the latest answer.
- **Smart Clipboard.** Open your local clipboard history with a shortcut. Search the history or filter to favorites, then click an entry or press Return to copy it while keeping the history open. You can also ask your configured model to edit a selected entry.
- **Layout improvements.** Settings and feature pages adapt to the window width to reduce large empty areas. Screen Awareness and Smart Clipboard answer panels fit their content and put the copy button in the upper-right corner.

Add and enable Screen Awareness and Smart Clipboard in Feature Center. To update, open Settings → About & updates and check for updates. For a fresh installation, [download the macOS installer for Apple silicon](https://github.com/bohu8264/NuPhyLab-next/releases/latest/download/NuPhyLab-macOS-AppleSilicon.dmg).

## 简体中文

- **我的指令。** 在智能框选中保存自己的指令，用快捷键处理选中文字。可以核对原文和处理要求，继续追问，并在结果就绪后复制。
- **AI 屏幕感知。** 用文字或语音提问。默认读取鼠标所在显示器的可见窗口，也可以选择单个窗口或框选区域。提问前可预览截图，收到回答后可继续追问，或复制最近一次回答。
- **智能剪贴板。** 用快捷键打开本机剪贴板历史，搜索记录或筛选收藏。点选记录或按回车即可复制，历史窗口继续显示。也可以让已配置的模型处理所选记录。
- **布局改进。** 设置和功能页面随窗口宽度调整，减少大块留白。AI 屏幕感知和智能剪贴板的回答区随内容收放，复制按钮位于右上角。

在「功能中心」分别添加并启用 AI 屏幕感知和智能剪贴板。已经安装的用户可在「设置 → 关于与更新」检查更新。首次安装可[下载适用于 Apple 芯片的 macOS 安装包](https://github.com/bohu8264/NuPhyLab-next/releases/latest/download/NuPhyLab-macOS-AppleSilicon.dmg)。
