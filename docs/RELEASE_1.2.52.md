# NuPhy Lab 1.2.52

## 中文

- 在功能中心添加「取色器」，点选屏幕颜色即可复制 HEX 色号。可以用一个单键、组合键或鼠标侧键取色。重新录制会替换原键，取色结果会在屏幕顶部短暂显示。
- 语音控制的指令快捷键支持鼠标侧键。点当前键位，再按下并松开想用的侧键。按一次开始说指令，再按一次执行。
- 点键位旁的 × 可以清除指令快捷键。这个选择会在重新打开 NuPhy Lab 后保留，也可以直接点「说指令」使用语音控制。

在 **功能中心** 添加 **取色器**，点 **开始取色** 后选中屏幕上的颜色。大写 sRGB HEX 色号会复制到剪贴板，并在屏幕顶部短暂显示。按 Esc 取消会保留原剪贴板。

点击 **取色快捷键** 下的键位，按下并松开要使用的单键、组合键或鼠标侧键，再打开 **全局快捷键**。取色器只保存一个键位，重新录制会替换它，点 × 可清空。清空后仍可点 **开始取色**。

要修改语音指令键，打开页首的 **语音控制设置**，点当前键位后录制鼠标侧键。点 × 清空指令键后，仍可点 **说指令**。

## English

- Add Color Picker from Feature Center to sample a screen color and copy its HEX code. Set a single key, key combination or mouse side button as your shortcut. Recording again replaces it. The result appears briefly at the top of the screen.
- Voice Control supports mouse side buttons. Click the current shortcut, then press and release the side button you want. Press once to speak and again to run the command.
- Click × beside the shortcut to clear it. Your choice is saved when you reopen NuPhy Lab. You can still click Speak a command to use Voice Control.

Add **Color Picker** in **Feature Center**, click **Pick a color**, then select a color on the screen. NuPhy Lab copies the uppercase sRGB HEX code and shows it briefly at the top of the screen. Press Esc to cancel and keep the current clipboard.

Click the displayed **Color picker shortcut**, press and release a key, key combination or mouse side button, then turn on **Global shortcut**. Color Picker saves one binding. Recording again replaces it, and clicking × clears it. You can still click **Pick a color** with the shortcut cleared.

To change the command shortcut, open **Voice Control settings** in the page header, click the current binding and record a mouse side button. You can click × to clear the command shortcut and continue using **Speak a command**.
