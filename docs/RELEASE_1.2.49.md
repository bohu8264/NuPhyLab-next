# NuPhy Lab 1.2.49

## 中文

- 语音输入支持点击录制鼠标侧键，按一次开始，再按一次结束。
- 新增与智能框选侧键的冲突提示，保留原快捷键和语音设置。
- 语音未能插入输入框时自动复制结果，可直接粘贴。
- 移回功能中心会关闭功能及快捷键，重新添加保持关闭，保留原内容和配置。

在语音输入页面点击快捷键按钮，按下并松开所选侧键即可保存。支持系统可识别的鼠标侧键；左键、右键和中键保持原用途。已有键盘快捷键、词库、整理提示词、麦克风及服务设置会保留。

没有输入框或无法自动插入时，完成的文字自动复制到剪贴板，并提示 ⌘V 粘贴。若等待插入期间你复制了其他内容，软件保留新剪贴板，语音结果仍在页面供复制。

功能移回功能中心后，相关功能和快捷键关闭，作品、历史和配置保留；重新添加后需按需开启。

## English

- Voice Input supports recording a mouse side button: click once to start and again to finish.
- Conflicts with Smart Selection side buttons are explained while existing shortcuts and voice settings are preserved.
- When speech cannot be inserted into a text field, the result is copied for manual pasting.
- Removing a tool turns it and its shortcuts off. Re-adding keeps it off and preserves its content and preferences.

On the Voice Input page, click the shortcut control, then press and release a mouse side button to save it. System-recognized side buttons are supported; left, right and middle buttons retain their usual roles. Existing shortcuts, dictionary, refinement instructions, microphone and service settings are preserved.

If no text field is found or automatic insertion is unavailable, the completed result is copied and a ⌘V notice appears. A newer copy made while waiting to insert is preserved; the speech result remains on the page for manual copying.

Removing a tool in Feature Center switches off its functionality and shortcuts while preserving works, history and preferences. Re-add it and enable it when needed.
